# Loyalty Program

## Earning Points

You earn 1 point for every $1 spent on eligible purchases. Points are credited after the order ships or is delivered. Cancelled and returned orders do not earn points.

## Tiers

- **Bronze**: 0–499 points
- **Silver**: 500–1,999 points (5% off future orders)
- **Gold**: 2,000–4,999 points (10% off future orders + free standard shipping)
- **Platinum**: 5,000+ points (15% off future orders + free expedited shipping + priority support)

Tier status is evaluated after each completed order. Once you reach a tier, you keep it for the current calendar year and the following year.

## Redeeming Points

Points can be redeemed at checkout. 100 points = $1 off. You can redeem any amount over 500 points in increments of 100.

## Points Expiration

Points expire if there is no earning or redemption activity for 24 consecutive months.

## Referrals

Earn 200 bonus points when a referred friend completes their first purchase. The friend also receives a $10 welcome discount.
