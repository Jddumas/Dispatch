# Frequently Asked Questions

## Orders

**How do I track my order?**
Log in to your account and go to Order History. Click any order to see its current status and tracking number. You can also enter your tracking number directly on the carrier's website.

**Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?**
You can cancel within 1 hour of placing the order. After that, the order enters fulfillment and cannot be changed. If the order has already shipped, you will need to wait for delivery and then start a return.

**What if I received the wrong item?**
Contact support immediately. We will ship the correct item at no cost and provide a prepaid return label for the wrong item. You do not need to return it before the replacement ships.

**How long does shipping take?**
Standard shipping takes 5–7 business days. Express shipping takes 2 business days. Orders over $35 qualify for free standard shipping.

## Returns and Refunds

**What is the return window?**
30 days from the delivery date. The item must be unused and in its original packaging.

**What items cannot be returned?**
Digital downloads, gift cards, and items marked as final sale cannot be returned.

**How long does a refund take?**
5–7 business days after the warehouse receives your return. Bank transfers may take up to 10 business days.

**Can I return an item after 30 days?**
In most cases no. Exceptions are made for defective items covered under warranty. Contact support to open a warranty claim instead of a return.

**What if my item arrived damaged?**
Contact support within 7 days of delivery with photos of the damage. We will send a replacement or issue a full refund without requiring you to return the item.

## Loyalty Program

**How do I earn points?**
You earn 1 point for every $1 spent. Points are credited after your order ships.

**When do my points expire?**
Points expire after 24 months of no earning or redemption activity.

**Can I use points and a discount code together?**
Yes. Points redemptions and promo codes can be stacked. Points redemptions and Subscribe & Save discounts can also be combined. However, only one promo code can be applied per order.

**How do I redeem my points?**
At checkout, enter the number of points you want to apply (minimum 500, in increments of 100). 100 points = $1 off.

## Account

**How do I reset my password?**
On the login page, click "Forgot password" and enter your email. A reset link valid for 24 hours will be sent. Check your spam folder if you don't see it.

**What if I no longer have access to my account email?**
Contact support and verify your identity with your order number and the billing address on file. We can transfer your account to a new email address.

**How do I enable two-factor authentication?**
Go to Account Settings > Security > Two-Factor Authentication. You can use an authenticator app or SMS.
