# Contact Support

You can reach our support team through live chat, email, or phone. Live chat is available Monday through Friday from 8 AM to 8 PM Central Time and Saturday from 10 AM to 4 PM Central Time.

Email responses are typically sent within one business day. For urgent issues such as missing or damaged orders, use live chat or phone for faster assistance.

Before contacting support, please have your order number or account email ready. This helps us locate your account and resolve the issue quickly.
