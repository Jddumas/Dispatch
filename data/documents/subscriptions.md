# Subscriptions and Plans

## Subscribe & Save

Many products are available on a Subscribe & Save plan. Choose a delivery frequency (every 30, 60, or 90 days) and save 10% on each shipment.

## Managing Subscriptions

You can pause, skip, or cancel a subscription at any time from Account Settings > Subscriptions. Changes must be made at least 2 days before the next scheduled shipment.

## Billing Schedule

Subscriptions are billed automatically on the day the order is processed. You will receive an email confirmation each time a subscription order ships.

## Subscription Discounts

The Subscribe & Save discount stacks with loyalty tier benefits but cannot be combined with one-time promo codes.

## Warranty Extension

Some plans include an extended warranty. If your subscription includes an extended warranty, coverage begins after the manufacturer's warranty expires and lasts for the duration of the subscription.
