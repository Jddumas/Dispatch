# Loyalty Points Redemption and Stacking Guide

## How to Redeem Points

At checkout, you will see a "Use Points" section showing your available balance. Enter the number of points to apply (minimum 500, increments of 100). The discount is applied immediately before payment.

**Example**: 1,200 points = $12 off. You can apply 1,200, 1,100, 1,000, etc. — any multiple of 100 above 500.

## Stacking Discounts

Points can be combined with other offers in the following ways:

| Combination | Allowed? |
|---|---|
| Points + promo code | Yes |
| Points + Subscribe & Save discount | Yes |
| Points + tier discount (Gold/Platinum) | Yes |
| Two promo codes on one order | No — only one promo code per order |
| Points + gift card | Yes |

**Example**: A Platinum member (15% tier discount) using Subscribe & Save (10%) and 500 loyalty points ($5 off) on a $100 item would pay approximately $75 after all discounts.

## Tier Discount Details

Tier discounts apply automatically at checkout based on your current tier:

- **Bronze** (0–499 pts): No automatic discount
- **Silver** (500–1,999 pts): 5% off all orders
- **Gold** (2,000–4,999 pts): 10% off + free standard shipping on every order
- **Platinum** (5,000+ pts): 15% off + free expedited shipping + priority support queue

Tier status is evaluated after each completed order and held for the rest of the current calendar year plus the following year.

## How to Reach the Next Tier

Your points balance determines your tier. To move from Gold to Platinum you need 5,000 total lifetime points earned.

**Example**: If you have 3,800 points, you need 1,200 more to reach Platinum. At $1 per point, that means $1,200 more in eligible purchases.

## Points That Don't Count

Points are not earned on:
- Returned or cancelled orders
- Amounts paid with points redemptions (you don't earn points on the discounted portion)
- Shipping fees
- Gift card purchases

## Referral Bonus

When a friend signs up using your referral link and completes their first purchase, you earn 200 bonus points and they receive a $10 welcome discount. There is no limit to how many referrals you can make.

## Points Expiration

Points expire after 24 consecutive months of no earning or redemption activity. A single eligible purchase or redemption resets the 24-month clock for all points in your account.
