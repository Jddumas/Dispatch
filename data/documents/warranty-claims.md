# Warranty Claims Guide

## What Is Covered

Most products include a one-year limited warranty from the date of purchase. Coverage includes:

- Manufacturing defects in materials or workmanship
- Hardware failures under normal use (e.g., screen failure, battery not holding charge, broken buttons)
- Components that fail before the expected lifespan

## What Is NOT Covered

- Accidental damage (drops, spills, physical impact)
- Water or liquid damage
- Normal wear and tear (scratches, faded color, worn cable insulation)
- Damage from unauthorized repairs or modifications
- Damage caused by using incompatible accessories or chargers
- Products purchased from unauthorized third-party sellers

## How to File a Claim

1. **Gather your information**: Order number, date of purchase, and a description of the defect.
2. **Contact support**: Open a ticket or use live chat. Describe the problem clearly and attach photos or a short video if possible.
3. **Review**: A support agent will review your claim within 1–2 business days. They may ask for additional photos or a video demonstration.
4. **Resolution**: If approved, we will either send a replacement or issue a full refund. For approved claims, we provide a prepaid return label.
5. **Return the defective item**: Ship the item back using the prepaid label within 14 days of receiving it.

## Typical Resolution Times

- Claim review: 1–2 business days
- Replacement shipped: 2–3 business days after approval
- Refund processed: 5–7 business days after the defective item is received

## Extended Warranty

Extended warranties of 1 or 2 additional years are available at checkout for select products. Extended warranties cover the same defects as the standard warranty and activate after the original 1-year warranty expires.

Extended warranty coverage also includes one free battery replacement for applicable devices (laptops, wireless headphones, smartwatches) during the extended period.

## If Your Claim Is Denied

If your claim is denied, you can request a second review by a senior support agent. Provide any additional evidence (e.g., photos showing no physical damage, original packaging) to support your case.

If the denial is upheld, the item can be sent for a paid repair estimate. Repair costs vary by product and defect.

## Warranty for Gifted Items

If you received the product as a gift, the warranty still applies. Provide the order number from the original purchase (ask the gift giver) and your name and address for the replacement shipment.
