# Refunds

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after the returned item is received and inspected. If you paid with a gift card, the refund will be applied to your store credit balance.

Original shipping charges are refunded only if the return is due to a defective or incorrect item. If you paid for express shipping, that charge is not refundable unless the item was damaged or wrong.

You will receive an email notification once the refund is processed.
