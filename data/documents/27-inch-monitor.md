# 27-inch Monitor

The 27-inch monitor features a 2560 x 1440 QHD IPS panel with a 75 Hz refresh rate and 99% sRGB color coverage. It includes HDMI, DisplayPort, and USB-C inputs with 65W power delivery.

The stand supports height, tilt, swivel, and pivot adjustments. VESA 100 x 100 mounting is supported.

Brightness is rated at 350 nits and the response time is 5 ms. The monitor comes with a three-year warranty that covers dead pixels and backlight issues.
