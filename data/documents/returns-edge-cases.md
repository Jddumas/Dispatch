# Return Edge Cases and Exceptions

## Late Returns (After 30 Days)

The standard 30-day return window is firm for change-of-mind returns. However, exceptions are made in the following situations:

- **Defective items**: If a manufacturing defect appears within the warranty period (1 year), file a warranty claim instead of a return. Warranty claims do not require the item to be within the 30-day window.
- **Late delivery**: If the item arrived more than 7 days late due to a carrier issue, the 30-day window is extended to begin from the actual delivery date.
- **Gift returns**: Items received as gifts can be returned up to 60 days after the order date. The refund will be issued as store credit to the recipient's account.

To request a late return exception, contact support and explain the situation. Exceptions are reviewed case by case.

## Final Sale Items

Items marked "final sale" at the time of purchase cannot be returned or exchanged under any circumstances. This includes clearance items, open-box products, and seasonal promotions marked as final sale. The product page will clearly indicate if an item is final sale before purchase.

## International Orders

International orders follow the same 30-day return window, but the customer is responsible for return shipping costs. We do not provide prepaid return labels for international returns. Import duties and taxes paid at delivery are non-refundable.

## Damaged or Defective on Arrival

If your item arrived damaged or defective:
1. Take photos of the damage and the packaging.
2. Contact support within 7 days of delivery.
3. We will ship a replacement immediately without requiring the return first.
4. A prepaid return label for the damaged item will be sent separately.

You do not need to wait for the replacement to arrive before reporting the issue.

## Items That Cannot Be Returned

- Digital downloads and software licenses (non-refundable once the download link is accessed)
- Gift cards and store credit
- Items marked as final sale
- Perishable or consumable goods
- Items that have been used, washed, or had tags removed
- Products returned without original packaging

## Partial Returns

If your order contained multiple items, you can return individual items. Each item's 30-day window starts from the delivery date of that order.

## Refund to a Different Payment Method

Refunds are always issued to the original payment method. If that card has been closed or expired, the bank will typically redirect the refund to your new account. If not, contact support and we will issue a check or store credit instead.
