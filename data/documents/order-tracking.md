# Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you will receive an email with a tracking number and a link to the carrier's tracking page. You can also track your order from the Orders tab in your account.

Tracking information may take up to 24 hours to update after the carrier receives the package. If your tracking number does not show movement after 48 hours, contact support for assistance.

For orders with multiple items, each shipment may have its own tracking number.
