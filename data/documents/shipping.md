# Shipping Information

Standard shipping is free for orders over $35 and typically takes 3-5 business days. Orders under $35 have a flat $5 shipping fee.

Express shipping is available for $15 and delivers within 1-2 business days. Express orders placed before 2 PM local time ship the same day.

International orders ship within 7-14 business days and may be subject to customs duties and taxes in the destination country. Tracking information is emailed once the order ships.
