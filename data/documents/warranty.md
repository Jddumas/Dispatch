# Warranty Coverage

Most products include a one-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects from the date of purchase. The warranty covers repair or replacement of defective parts but does not cover accidental damage, water damage, or normal wear and tear.

To file a warranty claim, provide your order number and a description of the defect. A support agent will review the claim and provide a prepaid return label if the claim is approved.

Extended warranties are available at checkout for select products.
