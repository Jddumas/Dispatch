# Account Security

## Strong Passwords

Use a unique password with at least 12 characters, including uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols. Avoid reusing passwords from other sites.

## Two-Factor Authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds an extra layer of security. Enable 2FA from Account Settings > Security. We support authenticator apps and SMS codes.

## Suspicious Activity

If you notice orders, address changes, or password reset emails you did not initiate, change your password immediately and contact support. We can review recent account activity and lock the account if needed.

## Password Resets

You can request a password reset from the login page. Reset links expire after 1 hour and can only be used once. If you do not receive the email within a few minutes, check your spam folder.

## Account Deactivation

You can deactivate your account from Account Settings. Deactivation hides your profile but keeps order history for legal and billing purposes. To permanently delete your data, contact support.
