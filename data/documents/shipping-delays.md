# Shipping Delays and Lost Packages

## My Order Is Late — What Do I Do?

First, check the tracking number in your Order History. Common reasons for delays:

- **In transit**: The package is moving but the carrier hasn't updated the scan yet. Wait 1 business day.
- **Out for delivery**: The package will arrive today. Carriers sometimes deliver as late as 9 PM.
- **Weather or high-volume delay**: Carriers may delay packages during peak periods (holidays, severe weather). This is outside our control.
- **Stuck at a facility**: If the tracking shows the same location for more than 3 business days, contact support.

## When to Contact Support

Contact us if:
- Standard shipping order is more than 7 business days past the estimated delivery date
- Express shipping order is more than 2 business days past the estimated date
- Tracking shows "Delivery Exception" or "Return to Sender"
- Tracking hasn't updated in 5+ business days

## What Happens When You Contact Support

A support agent will:
1. Open a trace request with the carrier (takes 1–3 business days for the carrier to investigate)
2. If the carrier confirms the package is lost, we will reship the order at no cost or issue a full refund — your choice
3. If the package is found during the trace, delivery will resume

## Delivered But Not Received (Porch Theft)

If tracking shows "Delivered" but you did not receive the package:

1. Check with neighbors and any secondary delivery locations (mailroom, front desk, side door)
2. Wait 24 hours — carriers sometimes mark packages as delivered 1 day early
3. If still not found after 24 hours, contact support. We will open a carrier investigation.
4. We will reship or refund once the investigation confirms non-delivery (typically 3–5 business days)

## Wrong Address

If your order was shipped to the wrong address due to our error, we will reship to the correct address immediately. If the wrong address was entered by you at checkout, contact support as soon as possible. If the package hasn't been picked up by the carrier yet, we can update it; otherwise you may need to intercept it yourself through the carrier's redirect service.

## International Shipments

International orders may be held at customs. You may be required to pay import duties before the package is released. Dispatch is not responsible for customs delays or import fees. If your package has been at customs for more than 14 business days, contact support.

## Requesting a Refund for a Late Order

If your order arrives more than 7 days past the estimated delivery date, you are eligible for a refund of your shipping fee (if paid). Contact support with your order number to request the shipping refund.
