# Password Reset

If you forgot your password, go to the login page and click "Forgot Password." Enter the email address associated with your account and we will send a reset link.

The reset link expires after 24 hours for security reasons. If you do not receive the email within a few minutes, check your spam folder or verify that you entered the correct email address.

For additional security, we recommend using a password manager and enabling two-factor authentication in Account Settings.
