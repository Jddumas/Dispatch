# Account Troubleshooting

## I Forgot My Password

1. Go to the login page and click "Forgot password"
2. Enter the email address associated with your account
3. Check your inbox for a reset link (valid for 24 hours)
4. If you don't see it within 5 minutes, check your spam or junk folder
5. Click the link, enter a new password, and log in

If you no longer have access to that email address, see "I lost access to my account email" below.

## I Forgot Which Email I Used

Try all email addresses you commonly use. If you have an order confirmation email, the "To:" field shows the account email.

If you cannot find it, contact support with your full name, billing address, and a past order number. We can look up your account and send the reset link to your verified email.

## I Lost Access to My Account Email

Contact support and provide:
- Your full name
- The billing address on your most recent order
- The last 4 digits of the payment method used

After verifying your identity, a support agent can update the email address on your account within 1 business day.

## My Account Is Locked

Accounts are temporarily locked after 10 consecutive failed login attempts. The lock expires automatically after 30 minutes.

If you believe your account was locked due to unauthorized access attempts, contact support immediately. We can lock the account for security, review recent activity, and help you regain access.

## I Think Someone Else Accessed My Account

1. Change your password immediately (if you still have access)
2. Review your Order History for any orders you did not place
3. Go to Account Settings > Security and check for unfamiliar devices or sessions
4. Enable two-factor authentication to prevent future unauthorized access
5. Contact support to report the suspected breach — we will flag the account and investigate

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) Issues

**Lost access to your authenticator app**: Contact support and verify your identity. We can disable 2FA on your account so you can log in and set it up again with a new device.

**Not receiving SMS codes**: Check that your phone number is correct in Account Settings. If you recently changed your number, update it and request a new code. In areas with poor signal, SMS may be delayed by a few minutes.

## I Cannot Update My Payment Method

- Ensure the card is not expired and the billing address matches what your bank has on file
- Prepaid cards and some debit cards are not accepted
- If the error persists, try a different browser or clear your browser cache

## My Account Was Deactivated

If you requested deactivation: accounts can be reactivated within 90 days by contacting support. After 90 days, the account is permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

If your account was deactivated without your request, contact support immediately with your order history for identity verification.
