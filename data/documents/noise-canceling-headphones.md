# Noise-Canceling Headphones

These over-ear headphones feature active noise cancellation and up to 30 hours of battery life. A 10-minute charge provides 4 hours of playback via fast charging.

They connect via Bluetooth 5.2 or the included 3.5mm audio cable. The headphones support multipoint connection, allowing you to connect to two devices at once.

The ear cushions are replaceable and the headphones fold flat for travel. The carrying case and a USB-C charging cable are included in the box.
