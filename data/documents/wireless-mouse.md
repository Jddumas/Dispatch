# Wireless Mouse

The wireless mouse features a precision optical sensor with adjustable DPI settings from 800 to 2400. It connects via a 2.4 GHz USB receiver and provides a reliable range of up to 10 meters.

Battery life lasts up to 12 months on a single AA battery, depending on usage. A low-battery LED indicator blinks when the battery needs replacement.

Compatible with Windows, macOS, and Linux. No additional drivers are required; plug in the receiver and the mouse is ready to use.
