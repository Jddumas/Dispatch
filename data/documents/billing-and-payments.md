# Billing and Payments

## Accepted Payment Methods

We accept credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and bank transfers. Bank transfers may take 2-5 business days to clear.

## Payment Failures

If a payment fails, we will automatically retry the charge once after 24 hours. You will receive an email notification with instructions to update your payment method. Orders tied to a failed payment are held until payment clears.

## Invoices and Receipts

An order confirmation and payment receipt are emailed immediately after a successful transaction. You can also download invoices from the Orders tab in your account.

## Refunds to Payment Methods

Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Credit card and PayPal refunds typically appear within 3-7 business days. Bank transfer refunds may take up to 10 business days depending on your institution.

## Tax

Sales tax is calculated based on the shipping address and included in the order total at checkout.
