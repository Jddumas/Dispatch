# Product Comparison Guide

## Monitors: 27-inch Monitor vs. 4K Monitor

| Feature | 27-inch Monitor ($299.99) | 4K Monitor ($449.99) |
|---|---|---|
| Resolution | 1440p (2560×1440) | 4K (3840×2160) |
| Panel size | 27 inches | 27 inches |
| Refresh rate | 144Hz | 60Hz |
| Best for | Gaming, fast-paced work | Photo/video editing, design |
| GPU requirement | Mid-range GPU | High-end GPU recommended |
| Text sharpness | Good | Excellent |

**Choose the 27-inch Monitor if**: You play games or need high refresh rates for smooth motion.
**Choose the 4K Monitor if**: You edit photos or video, do graphic design, or want the sharpest possible text and image detail.

## Headphones: Noise-Canceling Headphones ($249.99)

The Noise-Canceling Headphones are our only over-ear wireless model with active noise cancellation (ANC). They are best for:

- Office use and open-plan environments
- Travel and commuting
- Focus work where blocking distractions is important

They are **not** recommended for:
- High-intensity workouts (not sweat-resistant)
- Gaming requiring surround sound (stereo only)

If you need a wired option or prioritize microphone quality for calls, consider the Streaming Microphone ($129.99) separately.

## Audio: Streaming Microphone vs. USB Microphone

| Feature | Streaming Microphone ($129.99) | USB Microphone ($79.99) |
|---|---|---|
| Polar pattern | Cardioid + bidirectional | Cardioid only |
| Sample rate | 24-bit / 96kHz | 16-bit / 48kHz |
| Gain control | Hardware knob | Software only |
| Best for | Streaming, podcasting, interviews | Video calls, basic recording |
| Included stand | Yes | No |

**Choose the Streaming Microphone if**: You record podcasts, stream, or need higher audio quality.
**Choose the USB Microphone if**: You primarily use it for video calls and want a simpler, lower-cost option.

## Gaming: Mechanical Keyboard vs. Gaming Mouse

These are complementary products, not alternatives. The Mechanical Keyboard ($119.99) pairs well with the Gaming Mouse ($59.99) for a complete gaming desk setup.

**Mechanical Keyboard highlights**: Cherry MX switches (choice of Red for linear or Brown for tactile), full RGB backlighting, USB-C connection, N-key rollover for simultaneous key presses.

**Gaming Mouse highlights**: 16,000 DPI optical sensor, 6 programmable buttons, 1.8m braided cable, 400 IPS tracking speed.

## Smart Home: Smart Bulb Kit vs. Smart Thermostat

Both connect to Wi-Fi and work with Alexa and Google Home, but they serve different needs.

| | Smart Bulb Kit ($89.99) | Smart Thermostat ($179.99) |
|---|---|---|
| Controls | Lighting | Heating/cooling |
| Setup time | 5 minutes | 30–60 minutes |
| Requires | Wi-Fi | Wi-Fi + compatible HVAC |
| Savings | Lower electricity from scheduling | Up to 15% on heating/cooling bills |

**Note**: The Smart Thermostat requires a C-wire (common wire) in your HVAC system. Check your current thermostat wiring before purchasing.

## Wearables: Smart Watch vs. Fitness Tracker

| Feature | Smart Watch ($199.99) | Fitness Tracker ($129.99) |
|---|---|---|
| Display | Full color touchscreen | Small OLED |
| App support | Yes (iOS + Android) | No |
| GPS | Built-in | Via phone |
| Battery life | 2 days | 7 days |
| Best for | All-day use, notifications, apps | Sleep tracking, step counting |

**Choose the Smart Watch if**: You want notifications, music control, and a full-featured wearable.
**Choose the Fitness Tracker if**: You prioritize battery life and simple health metrics.
