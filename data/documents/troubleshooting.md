# General Troubleshooting

If a device is not responding, first try restarting it and checking the power or battery level. For wireless devices, replace or recharge the batteries and ensure the USB receiver is firmly connected.

For Bluetooth connection issues, remove the device from your Bluetooth settings and pair it again. Make sure the device is in pairing mode and within range.

If a product is not recognized by your computer, try a different USB port or cable. Updating the operating system and device drivers can also resolve many issues.
